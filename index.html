Reading the cost history: Cost per message rose 30 → 54 → 115 → 141 EGP across 2023–2026. This span includes the March 2024 EGP devaluation (~31 → ~48+ per USD), sustained inflation, and rising Meta auction prices — a substantial share of the increase is structural market cost, not campaign performance. The 2024→2025 jump also coincides with a 3× spend scale-up (756K → 2.3M EGP), and larger budgets typically buy marginal reach at higher unit cost. These figures establish the trend line; they do not isolate performance from macro effects. Account coverage note: total 2024 media investment across all EGP accounts was 1,220,822 EGP (main 755,985 + Instagram 359,909 + secondary 104,928) plus $441 on a USD account — the secondary accounts were nearly inactive in 2023, 2025, and 2026 (verified against lifetime exports), so the main-account trend line remains like-for-like across years.

Aug–Dec averages index 1.13 — the remainder of 2026 falls in a seasonally stronger-than-average window, which supports the Q4-weighted Le Marché plan. Possible drivers (exhibition season, delivery-before-year-end demand) are consistent with the pattern but not proven from this data.
