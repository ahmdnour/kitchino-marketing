Reading the cost history: Cost per message rose 30 → 54 → 115 → 141 EGP across 2023–2026 (main-account basis; the 143 EGP headline CPL includes Google spend). This span includes the March 2024 EGP devaluation (~31 → ~48+ per USD), sustained inflation, and rising Meta auction prices — a substantial share of the increase is structural market cost, not campaign performance. The 2024→2025 jump also coincides with a 3× spend scale-up (756K → 2.3M EGP), and larger budgets typically buy marginal reach at higher unit cost. These figures establish the trend line; they do not isolate performance from macro effects. Account coverage note: total 2024 media investment across all EGP accounts was 1,220,822 EGP (main 755,985 + Instagram 359,909 + secondary 104,928) plus $441 on a USD account — the secondary accounts were nearly inactive in 2023, 2025, and 2026 (verified against lifetime exports), so the main-account trend line remains like-for-like across years.
Reading seasonality: October (1.46) and November (1.32) are consistently the strongest months; April (0.57) the weakest. The January index (1.19) is inflated by a single outlier month (Jan 2024 = 134); excluding it, January is near average. Aug–Dec averages index 1.13 — the remainder of 2026 falls in a seasonally stronger-than-average window, which supports the Q4-weighted Le Marché plan. Possible drivers (exhibition season, delivery-before-year-end demand) are consistent with the pattern but not proven from this data.

One detail per Reel · 15 seconds · "Save this for when you build your kitchen"
📊 Measure: Saves + rewatches

Hook A: "A kitchen delivered this week in New Cairo" — entry shot through the door
Hook B: "A 6-meter kitchen turned into all of this" — wide shot, then details
One continuous walking path · hook before second 5 is mandatory · 45s max
📊 Measure: DMs + average watch time

Hook: "The #1 question in our DMs:…" — question shown as large on-screen text

📊 Measure: DMs within 48h + new questions in comments
